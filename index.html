How the score is built

The model gives up to 70 points from satellite and map features. Records add up to 30: fire NOC, approved floors, electrical load and the Osmania study's high-risk wards. People inside at that hour adjust the result. Records are not used to train the model, so it can run on any city.

Every building, record and past fire here is generated from rules. The neighbourhoods are real; the geometry is simulated.
